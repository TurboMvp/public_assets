- **When to offer:** The boss says they are behind on WhatsApp, forgot to answer someone, or asks who is waiting on them.
- **Share URL:** https://app.heypana.ai/t/de5fa07b-73f6-4a38-86a3-7fc78416dce3/whatsapp

## Inbox
