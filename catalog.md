# Pana trick catalog

Curated Pana tricks, each installable from its share URL.

## WhatsApp
- **Pitch:** Pana reads your WhatsApp, tells you who is waiting on a reply and alerts you on what matters. Nothing is sent without your OK, and auto-replies stay off until you switch them on.
- **When to offer:** The boss says they are behind on WhatsApp, forgot to answer someone, asks who is waiting on them, or wants messages answered automatically.
- **Share URL:** https://app.heypana.ai/t/de5fa07b-73f6-4a38-86a3-7fc78416dce3/whatsapp

## Inbox
- **Pitch:** All your email inboxes in one place, showing only what needs you. Never deletes or moves anything.
- **When to offer:** The boss complains about email overload, juggling several accounts, or asks what in their email actually needs them.
- **Share URL:** https://app.heypana.ai/t/45d40adf-17ab-4a97-b8d2-e565ddb428e9/inbox

## People
- **Pitch:** A personal CRM: everyone you know, their birthdays, and a private note per person.
- **When to offer:** The boss forgets a birthday, asks you to remember something about a person, or asks who someone is or when they last talked.
- **Share URL:** https://app.heypana.ai/t/27e3fed9-5229-405f-ba4c-2e5db2141635/people

## Nutrition
- **Pitch:** Log meals from a photo or a sentence in chat, and see today against your goal plus the weekly trend.
- **When to offer:** The boss sends a meal photo, says what they ate, mentions a diet, weight or protein goal, or asks how they are doing on food.
- **Share URL:** https://app.heypana.ai/t/d86b42bc-6062-42f2-8b40-a2c0fda6eccc/nutrition

## Tasks
- **Pitch:** Your daily list, kept by Pana: say it once and Pana plans the day, carries over what is left and closes what it sees done.
- **When to offer:** The boss asks to be reminded of something, mentions things they keep meaning to do, asks what is on today or where to start, or says they are losing track.
- **Share URL:** https://app.heypana.ai/t/0ad053a8-6d44-4478-a9a0-4d84b228ffcf/tasks
